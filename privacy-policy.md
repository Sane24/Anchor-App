# Anchor — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

Anchor is a student project built for UC Berkeley coursework. This page explains what data Anchor accesses and how it's used.

## What we access

When you connect your Google account, Anchor requests **read-only** access to:

- **Google Calendar** — to show your events in your daily briefing
- **Gmail** — to show your unread messages as potential tasks in your daily briefing

Anchor never sends emails, creates or edits calendar events, or modifies your Google account in any way.

## How your data is used

- Calendar events and email subject lines are displayed only within the app, to help you plan your day.
- Data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and, if you create an Anchor account, in a private database record tied to your account.
- We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
- We do not use your data to train any AI models.

## Who can see your data

Only you. Each user's saved plans, journal entries, and focus sessions are private and protected so that other users cannot access them.

## Removing your data

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from Settings. You can also delete your saved Anchor data (plans, journal entries, focus sessions) from Settings.

## Contact

This is a student project, not a commercial product. Questions can be directed to the project team via the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Sane24/Anchor-App).
